Build and host validation

- Docker-backed QCOM compile: 88/88 A630 kernels compiled and survived the
  pickle round-trip. Each of the 88 binary payloads matched the packaged NS
  build byte-for-byte.
- Numerical replay: 27 pinned-tinygrad Metal policy calls across three input
  fixtures and nine recurrent frames, plus warp outputs at 1344x760 and
  1928x1208. All outputs were finite.

The Docker compile verifies the QCOM build and serialization. It does not
execute QCOM binaries because no A630 device is attached. Numerical replay used
the available Metal path, not the packaged QCOM binaries. Device execution on
the comma is still required; ONNX parity and driving behavior are unverified.
