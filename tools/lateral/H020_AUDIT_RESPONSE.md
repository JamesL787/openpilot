## Explicitly unresolved — calibration belongs to the other effort

1. **H020 command-map and loop-gain mismatch:** TargetMap-D is pasted onto H020's native nonlinear key axis. R5/key slopes are 8.5, 35.4, 19.1, 22.2, 16.8, 19.1, 15.4 and 13.7, versus 18.04 linear on Clarity. The independent calibration audit reports that the 10-16 PTM_FIX_FAMILY rev-b rebuild corrects eight other images, not H020. TGG-donor PID trims and load were fitted on a different image, so their loop gain does not transfer. Static feedforward inversion does not establish uniform residual-PID plant gain. No firmware rebuild, gain compensation, retune or calibration value change was made.
2. **Pre-table shaping:** H020 shaping gates and blend inputs remain unverified. Unity shaping remains an assumption; software tests do not establish runtime firmware gates.
3. **Installed PTM identity:** `ptm_build` remains an owner declaration, not an ECU readback. The EPS version cannot distinguish the supported tracker builds.
4. **Platform:** Bosch versus Nidec remains unverified without actual CarParams/carFw from this car. TEA appears under both Nidec Civic and Civic Bosch. Refusing an incompatible H020 activation and printing ECU match details does not establish its real platform.

No fitting algorithms, calibration values, firmware images or speculative fingerprint entries were changed because the user explicitly assigned calibration to another effort. R6 hardware assumptions and H020 timing/route validation also remain outside this implementation-only change. The implementation fixes do not make this a vehicle-validated calibration or establish readiness for a road test.
