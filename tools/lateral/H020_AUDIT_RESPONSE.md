## Explicitly unresolved — including after the later authorized rev-b candidate

1. **H020 command-map and loop-gain mismatch:** TargetMap-D is pasted onto H020's native nonlinear key axis. R5/key slopes are 8.5, 35.4, 19.1, 22.2, 16.8, 19.1, 15.4 and 13.7, versus 18.04 linear on Clarity. The independent calibration audit reports that the 10-16 PTM_FIX_FAMILY rev-b rebuild corrects eight other images, not H020. TGG-donor PID trims and load were fitted on a different image, so their loop gain does not transfer. Static feedforward inversion does not establish uniform residual-PID plant gain. No firmware rebuild, gain compensation, retune or calibration value change was made in the A–E commit. The later H02B candidate corrects the selected command/P data response to TGG rev-b and uses Clarity limits, with a distinct controller profile extracted from it. The legacy images still have the original mismatch. Neither this offline correction nor retained donor coefficients establishes H020 physical loop gain or validates calibration on H020.
2. **Pre-table shaping:** H020 shaping gates and blend inputs remain unverified. Unity shaping remains an assumption; software tests do not establish runtime firmware gates.
3. **Installed PTM identity:** `ptm_build` remains an owner declaration, not an ECU readback. The EPS version cannot distinguish tracker variants within H020/H02B. H02B has seven separately patched version records and a matching config family gate; live query behavior has not been observed.
4. **Platform:** Bosch versus Nidec remains unverified without actual CarParams/carFw from this car. TEA appears under both Nidec Civic and Civic Bosch. Refusing an incompatible H020 activation and printing ECU match details does not establish its real platform.

The initial calibration freeze was respected in `86aa8c485`. The subsequent explicit owner request authorized the new H02B firmware candidate and matching profile; the final choice was TGG response + Clarity limits. Fitting algorithms remain untouched and no stock/speculative ECU fingerprints were added. R6 hardware assumptions and H020 timing/route validation also remain unresolved after this follow-up. The implementation fixes do not make this a vehicle-validated calibration or establish readiness for a road test.
